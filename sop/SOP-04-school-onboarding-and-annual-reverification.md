Role codes (ED, FCD, CD, BC, QD, SC, RC, PO, IRC) are defined in the [SOP Manual §1.1](14-standard-operating-procedures.md#11-roles-used-in-this-manual). If this SOP conflicts with a policy, the policy controls.

## Procedure

1. **Conflict check.** The verifier confirms in writing they have no relationship with the school ([COI-001 §9.3](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md)).
2. **Collect evidence** using the **School Verification Checklist** (F-06):
   - TDOE listing showing Category I, II or III approval (screenshot or letter, dated);
   - current accreditation certificate from a Recognized Accrediting Agency (or Category II/III approval based on it);
   - evidence the school is an Islamic school (mission statement and curriculum);
   - Secretary of State good standing;
   - written admissions policy and nondiscrimination certification;
   - current tuition and required-fee schedule;
   - payment details, confirmed by phone with a known school contact.
3. **Sign the participation agreement** (F-07). Officer signatures per [FIN-001 §5](02-financial-controls-policy.md).
4. **Approve.** ED approves; CD checks the file is complete and signs F-06.
5. **Publish** the participating-school list on the website.
6. **Annual reverification.** Repeat step 2 for every school by March 1. Missing items are requested with a 15-business-day deadline.
7. **Status change.** If a school reports or TENNISSA learns of a change, apply [SCH-001 §4.3](01-scholarship-policy.md) immediately: suspend new awards, notify affected families in writing, consult counsel on committed awards.

## Controls

No award to a student at a school without a current, signed F-06 for that award year.
