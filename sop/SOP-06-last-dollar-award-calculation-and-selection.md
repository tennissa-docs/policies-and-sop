## Procedure

1. **Confirm the budget.** FCD confirms funds available for awards from the Section 25F account and the SOP-09 schedule. The Board-approved tiers apply.
2. **Sort by priority:** (1) renewals, (2) siblings of prior recipients, (3) all others.
3. **Rank within priority 3** using the published formula: lowest income as % of limit first; then highest Remaining Need as % of Qualified Costs; then the published tie-breaker.
4. **Calculate each award** on the **Award Calculation Sheet** (F-09):

| Line | Item |
|---|---|
| A | Qualified Costs (tuition + required fees from the school's schedule) |
| B | Other Assistance (EFS/ESA/IEA + school aid + other scholarships + other grants) |
| C | Remaining Need = A − B (not less than 0) |
| D | Tier maximum for the student's income band |
| E | **Award = lesser of C and D** (and within funds available) |

   If an EFS/ESA/IEA or school-aid decision is pending, use a documented estimate and mark the award **provisional**.
5. **Fill awards** in priority and rank order until funds are committed. Keep a ranked **waitlist**.
6. **Breadth check.** Confirm at least 10 recipients and more than one school. If not, CD alerts the Board before approval.
7. **Committee approval.** At least two conflict-free members approve the list. Members with a relationship to any applicant step out for that applicant; record it.
8. **Notify** recipients and families in writing within 10 business days: amount, term schedule, conditions, appeal rights. Notify schools of award amounts only.
9. **Adjustments.** If Other Assistance changes, recalculate on F-09, notify the family and school, and adjust remaining payments (SOP-07).

## Controls

Every award must trace to a completed F-09. The total awarded never exceeds funds confirmed in step 1.

## Records

`04-Scholarships/<year>/Awards/`, committee approval minutes.
