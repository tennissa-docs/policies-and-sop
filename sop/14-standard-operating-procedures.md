> **Plain-language summary.** The policies say *what* TENNISSA must do. These procedures say *how*, step by step, who does each step, who checks it, and what record proves it was done. If everyone follows the same steps, TENNISSA gets the same result every time, and an auditor can see it.

---

## 1. How to Use This Manual

1. **Policies control.** If an SOP conflicts with a policy, the policy wins and the SOP owner fixes the SOP.
2. **Every SOP has the same parts:** purpose, owner, checker, when it runs, steps, controls, and the record that proves completion.
3. **Maker and checker.** Wherever possible, the person who does a task (**Maker**) is not the person who reviews it (**Checker**). With a small team, a disinterested director or outside bookkeeper may serve as Checker.
4. **No record, not done.** A step counts as complete only when its record is saved in the location named in the SOP.
5. **Exceptions.** Any departure from an SOP is approved in advance by the SOP owner (or, for the owner's own tasks, by another officer), written in the **Exception Log** (Form F-20), and reported to the Board at the next meeting.

## SOP-01 Gift Intake and Deposit

**Purpose:** Record every gift the same way and put it in the right account.
**Owner:** FCD · **Checker:** ED · **When:** Each gift received; checks deposited at least weekly.

**Steps**

1. **Receive.** Online gifts come through the approved donation platform. Checks are opened by someone other than the person who records them in the books, where staffing allows, and logged on the **Check Log** (F-01) the day received.
2. **Classify.** Apply the decision table below.

| Question | If yes | If no |
|---|---|---|
| Is TENNISSA currently on the IRS SGO list for this year? ([DON-001 §3.2](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md)) | Continue | Non-qualified gift → Operating account |
| Is it cash (not stock, crypto, property or services)? | Continue | Non-qualified → Operating account (noncash: see DON-001 §8) |
| Is it from an individual in their own name? | Continue | Non-qualified → Operating account |
| Did the donor designate it as a qualified contribution **at the time of the gift**? | Continue | Non-qualified → Operating account |
| Is it free of any student earmark and of any goods or services in return? | **Qualified → Section 25F account** | Stop. Escalate to ED (possible refusal or refund, DON-001 §§4, 11) |

3. **Record** in the **Gift Log** (F-02): date, amount, method, donor name and address, qualified designation (Y/N) and the evidence (form checkbox, signed form or email), restriction, and any goods or services provided.
4. **Deposit.** Qualified gifts go to the Section 25F account **at full value**; processing fees are charged to the operating account. Confirm the platform settlement does the same.
5. **Large donor check.** If a donor's qualified gifts this year exceed $5,000, send the record to SOP-03 the same week.
6. **$250+ single gifts.** Queue a contemporaneous acknowledgment (send within 30 days; may be combined with SOP-02).
7. **Weekly match.** Each week, FCD matches the Gift Log, platform report and bank deposits. Differences are resolved within 5 business days.

**Controls:** No gift is moved between the Section 25F and operating accounts after deposit without a written CD/FCD decision on Form F-20. Designations are never changed after the gift.
**Record:** `03-Donors/<year>/` Gift Log, Check Log, designation evidence; `06-Finance/<year>/` deposit records.

## SOP-02 Donor Acknowledgments and IRS Contribution Reporting

**Purpose:** Give every qualified donor a correct, on-time acknowledgment and report the same numbers to the IRS.
**Owner:** CD · **Checker:** FCD · **When:** Prepare January 2–20; send by **January 31**; IRS portal report by **February 28**.

**Steps**

1. **January 2.** FCD closes the prior-year Gift Log and reconciles total qualified gifts to the Section 25F account deposits. Any difference is resolved before step 2.
2. **January 5.** CD generates each qualified donor's **unique donor number** using the IRS SGO portal instructions and records it in the Gift Log.
3. **January 10.** CD prepares acknowledgments from the approved template (F-03), showing: TENNISSA's EIN, total designated qualified contributions for the year, the unique donor number, and the goods-and-services statement.
4. **January 15.** FCD checks a sample of at least 10% of acknowledgments (minimum 10, or all if fewer) against the Gift Log and bank records, and signs the **Acknowledgment Check Sheet** (F-04). Any error means the full batch is rechecked.
5. **By January 31.** Send acknowledgments by email (where the donor consented to electronic delivery) or mail. Log the date sent for each donor.
6. **February 1–20.** CD prepares the portal upload (donor name, address, total qualified contributions per unique donor number). FCD confirms the total matches step 1.
7. **By February 28.** CD submits through the IRS SGO portal and saves the confirmation.
8. **Corrections.** Any later error follows [SGO-001 §5](08-section-25f-compliance-policy.md): report to ED and BC within 5 business days, then issue a corrected acknowledgment and portal amendment.

**Controls:** Acknowledgment totals, portal totals and Section 25F deposits must agree exactly. No acknowledgment states a tax result.
**Record:** `03-Donors/<year>/Acknowledgments/`, F-04, portal confirmation in `02-Compliance-25F/<year>/`.

## SOP-03 Disqualified-Person Register

**Purpose:** Make sure no scholarship ever goes to a disqualified person.
**Owner:** Board Chair's designee · **Checker:** CD · **When:** Every January; within 5 business days of any change.

**Steps**

1. **January.** Collect signed Appendix A statements ([COI-001](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md)) from every director, officer, Scholarship Committee member and reviewer.
2. **Identify substantial contributors** for the prior year from the Gift Log: any person (spouses combined) who gave more than $5,000 to the Section 25F account **and** more than 2% of that year's 25F contributions. Status lasts for that year and the next.
3. **List family members** of every disqualified person using the **Section 25F family definition**: spouse; ancestors and descendants of the person or spouse; siblings of the person or spouse; descendants of those siblings; spouses of any of these. Record names of school-age family members disclosed.
4. **Update the register** (F-05), dated and initialed by the designee and CD.
5. **Mid-year additions.** Add any new director, officer, committee member, reviewer or newly identified substantial contributor within 5 business days. If an award to someone now on the register is pending, stop it and notify CD.
6. **Screening.** Before any award is approved, SOP-05 step 7 checks every applicant household against the register.

**Controls:** The register is confidential, held in `01-Governance/Registers/`, and visible only to the designee, CD and the person running screening.
**Record:** F-05 with version date; signed Appendix A statements.

## SOP-04 School Onboarding and Annual Reverification

**Purpose:** Approve only schools that meet every requirement, and check them every year.
**Owner:** ED (or a conflict-free designee) · **Checker:** CD · **When:** New school requests; annually by **March 1** before each award year; whenever a change is reported.

**Steps**

1. **Conflict check.** The verifier confirms in writing they have no relationship with the school ([COI-001 §9.3](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md)).
2. **Collect evidence** using the **School Verification Checklist** (F-06):
   - TDOE listing showing Category I, II or III approval (screenshot or letter, dated);
   - current accreditation certificate from a Recognized Accrediting Agency (or Category II/III approval based on it);
   - evidence the school is an Islamic school (mission statement and curriculum);
   - Secretary of State good standing;
   - written admissions policy and nondiscrimination certification;
   - current tuition and required-fee schedule;
   - payment details, confirmed by phone with a known school contact.
3. **Sign the participation agreement** (F-07). Officer signatures per [FIN-001 §5](02-financial-controls-policy.md).
4. **Approve.** ED approves; CD checks the file is complete and signs F-06.
5. **Publish** the participating-school list on the website.
6. **Annual reverification.** Repeat step 2 for every school by March 1. Missing items are requested with a 15-business-day deadline.
7. **Status change.** If a school reports or TENNISSA learns of a change, apply [SCH-001 §4.3](01-scholarship-policy.md) immediately: suspend new awards, notify affected families in writing, consult counsel on committed awards.

**Controls:** No award to a student at a school without a current, signed F-06 for that award year.
**Record:** `05-Schools/<school>/<year>/`.

## SOP-05 Scholarship Application Intake and Eligibility Verification

**Purpose:** Verify every applicant the same way before any ranking.
**Owner:** ED · **Checker:** Scholarship Committee (sample review) · **When:** Each award cycle.

**Steps**

1. **Publish (before the window opens):** criteria, priority rules, ranking method, award tiers, deadline, required documents, and the privacy notice. Save a dated copy.
2. **Intake** through the approved platform only, with signed consents (data sharing with school; FERPA consent where needed).
3. **Completeness check** within 10 business days of receipt. Send one written request for missing items with a 10-business-day deadline. Incomplete files after the deadline are closed and logged.
4. **Residence and public-school eligibility.** Confirm Tennessee residence (for example, lease, utility bill or state ID) and school age, or a documented exception.
5. **Household income and family size** for the **prior calendar year**, using one method, recorded on the **Eligibility Worksheet** (F-08):
   - **Direct**: tax return or IRS transcript, W-2s, pay stubs, other cash income; compare to the IRS-published income limit for the area and family size; or
   - **Categorical**: benefit award letter dated within 12 months (SNAP, TANF, WIC, Section 8, SSI); or
   - **Foster-child safe harbor**.
   Redact Social Security numbers on upload; do not request them.
6. **Enrollment.** Confirm enrollment or acceptance at a participating school with a current F-06.
7. **Disqualified-person screen** against the register (SOP-03). Record the result and screener's initials.
8. **Other Assistance.** Record all EFS/ESA/IEA, school aid and other scholarships disclosed, with amounts and status (applied, awarded, received). Ask the school to confirm school aid.
9. **Eligibility decision.** Mark eligible / ineligible with reason. Ineligible applicants get a written notice with appeal rights ([SCH-001 §11](01-scholarship-policy.md)).
10. **Quality check.** A Scholarship Committee member re-verifies a random 10% of eligible files (minimum 5) before ranking. Any error triggers review of all files by that verifier.

**Controls:** A verifier with a relationship to an applicant does not touch that file. Same documents, same method, same deadline for everyone.
**Record:** Platform file plus F-08; export to `04-Scholarships/<year>/`.

## SOP-06 Last-Dollar Award Calculation and Selection

**Purpose:** Rank and size awards objectively and identically for everyone.
**Owner:** ED · **Checker:** Scholarship Committee · **When:** After SOP-05 is complete.

**Steps**

1. **Confirm the budget.** FCD confirms funds available for awards from the Section 25F account and the SOP-09 schedule. The Board-approved tiers apply.
2. **Sort by priority:** (1) renewals, (2) siblings of prior recipients, (3) all others.
3. **Rank within priority 3** using the published formula: lowest income as % of limit first; then highest Remaining Need as % of Qualified Costs; then the published tie-breaker.
4. **Calculate each award** on the **Award Calculation Sheet** (F-09):

| Line | Item |
|---|---|
| A | Qualified Costs (tuition + required fees from the school's schedule) |
| B | Other Assistance (EFS/ESA/IEA + school aid + other scholarships + other grants) |
| C | Remaining Need = A − B (not less than 0) |
| D | Tier maximum for the student's income band |
| E | **Award = lesser of C and D** (and within funds available) |

   If an EFS/ESA/IEA or school-aid decision is pending, use a documented estimate and mark the award **provisional**.
5. **Fill awards** in priority and rank order until funds are committed. Keep a ranked **waitlist**.
6. **Breadth check.** Confirm at least 10 recipients and more than one school. If not, CD alerts the Board before approval.
7. **Committee approval.** At least two conflict-free members approve the list. Members with a relationship to any applicant step out for that applicant; record it.
8. **Notify** recipients and families in writing within 10 business days: amount, term schedule, conditions, appeal rights. Notify schools of award amounts only.
9. **Adjustments.** If Other Assistance changes, recalculate on F-09, notify the family and school, and adjust remaining payments (SOP-07).

**Controls:** Every award must trace to a completed F-09. The total awarded never exceeds funds confirmed in step 1.
**Record:** `04-Scholarships/<year>/Awards/`, committee approval minutes.

## SOP-07 Scholarship Disbursement, Adjustments and Refunds

**Purpose:** Pay schools correctly, on time, and recover any overpayment.
**Owner:** FCD · **Checker:** ED (second authorizer) · **When:** Each term.

**Steps**

1. **Enrollment confirmation.** Before each term, the school confirms in writing (F-10) which recipients are enrolled, their current charges and any change in school aid.
2. **Prepare the payment batch** from approved F-09 amounts for the term, one line per student, totaled by school.
3. **Dual authorization.** FCD prepares; a second authorized signer approves in the bank system. Payments go only to school bank details verified under SOP-04.
4. **Send remittance** to each school listing students and amounts (no other personal data).
5. **School confirmation.** School confirms amounts were applied to student accounts within 15 business days.
6. **Withdrawals and overpayments.** When a student withdraws, changes school or Other Assistance increases, request a refund in writing within 5 business days. Track on the **Refund Log** (F-11).
7. **Deposit refunds** in the Section 25F account. Record the refund year (it is new income for SOP-09).
8. **Follow up** unpaid refunds at 30 and 60 days; escalate to ED at 60 days.

**Controls:** No payment to a parent or student. No payment without F-10 for that term.
**Record:** `06-Finance/<year>/Scholarship-Payments/`, F-10, F-11.

## SOP-08 Monthly Close and Bank Reconciliation

**Purpose:** Catch errors and fraud quickly; give the Board accurate numbers.
**Owner:** FCD (or outside bookkeeper) · **Checker:** A disinterested director or the bookkeeper, whoever did not prepare it · **When:** By the **15th** of each month.

**Steps**

1. Download statements for every account.
2. Reconcile each account to the books; list and explain every reconciling item.
3. Match Section 25F account deposits to the Gift Log (qualified gifts only) and withdrawals to approved scholarship batches only. **Any non-scholarship withdrawal from the 25F account is reported to the Board Chair immediately.**
4. Review all payments over $1,000 for two approvals.
5. Checker reviews, asks questions, and signs the **Reconciliation Sign-off** (F-12).
6. Prepare monthly statements by account for the Board, including account balances, institutions and earnings ([INV-001 §9](11-investment-policy.md)).

**Controls:** The preparer did not initiate or approve the month's payments, where possible. Unresolved items older than 30 days go to the Board.
**Record:** `06-Finance/<year>/<month>/`.

## SOP-09 90% Spending Schedule and 85% Activity Test

**Purpose:** Prove TENNISSA spends at least 90% of each year's income on scholarships on time.
**Owner:** FCD · **Checker:** CD · **When:** Quarterly (report at next Board meeting); annual test by **January 31**.

**Steps**

1. **Quarterly schedule** (F-13). For each receipt year, show:

| Receipt year | Income (qualified gifts + earnings + school refunds) | Scholarships paid against it (FIFO) | 90% target | Remaining to pay | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 | | | | | Dec 31, 2028 |
| 2028 | | | | | Dec 31, 2029 |

2. Apply payments to the **earliest year first**; count a scholarship only when paid; count each payment once.
3. **Red flag.** If any year's remaining amount cannot be paid from scheduled awards at least six months before its deadline, CD puts it on the next Board agenda with options (higher tiers, supplemental cycle).
4. **85% activity test (annual).** FCD and CD document the share of TENNISSA's activities that are scholarship-granting, using the method the CPA approves (for example, expenses or staff time), with the calculation saved. If the result is near or below 85%, alert the Board and CPA immediately ([FIN-001 §6.3](02-financial-controls-policy.md)).

**Controls:** CD re-performs the FIFO math each quarter; differences are resolved before the Board meeting.
**Record:** `02-Compliance-25F/<year>/Spending/`.

## SOP-10 Payments and Expense Reimbursement

**Purpose:** Every payment is approved, supported and from the right account.
**Owner:** Requester · **Checker:** Approver per [EXP-001 §5](09-expense-reimbursement-policy.md) and [FIN-001 §5](02-financial-controls-policy.md) · **When:** Each request.

**Steps**

1. **Pre-approval** for any trip or expense over $500, or any unbudgeted expense (F-14).
2. **Submit** the **Expense Report** (F-15) within 60 days with itemized receipts, business purpose, date, place, attendees for meals, and mileage log.
3. **Approver checks:** policy limits, receipts, budget line, no related-party purchase without COI approval, not the approver's own or a family member's expense.
4. **FCD codes** to the operating account. **Nothing is paid from the Section 25F account.**
5. **Pay** within 15 business days; two signers if over $1,000.
6. **Advances:** issued no more than 30 days before the expense; unused amounts returned within 120 days, tracked on F-15.

**Controls:** Late reports (over 60 days) need a written exception on F-20; employee late reimbursements are reported as wages.
**Record:** `06-Finance/<year>/Payments/`.

## SOP-11 Payroll, Timekeeping and Compensation Approval

**Purpose:** Pay people correctly, only as approved, and protect the § 4958 rebuttable presumption.
**Owner:** FCD · **Checker:** Qualified directors (compensation); ED (timesheets of other staff) · **When:** Each pay period; compensation annually or on change.

**Steps — compensation approval (annual or on change)**

1. ED (or the QD for the ED) gathers **comparability data**: at least three comparable positions from similar nonprofits, a survey or written offers (F-16).
2. QD meet **without** the person concerned. At least two QD vote in favor ([COI-001 §7.5](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md)).
3. Minutes record terms (fixed salary or hourly rate), period, data relied on, directors present and votes, conflicts disclosed. Minutes finalized within 60 days.
4. FCD updates payroll only from the signed resolution. No retroactive or formula pay.

**Steps — each pay period**

1. Non-exempt employees submit timesheets showing daily hours; supervisor approves. For an officer, a QD approves.
2. FCD checks the 40-hour week and 130-hour month limits. Overtime needs prior written Board approval.
3. Run payroll from the operating account with withholding; save the register.

**Controls:** Payroll rates must match the latest compensation resolution exactly; FCD confirms each run.
**Record:** `07-People/Payroll/<year>/`; resolutions in `01-Governance/`.

## SOP-12 Board Meetings, Conflict Disclosures and Minutes

**Purpose:** Make decisions that are lawful, documented and free of conflicts.
**Owner:** Secretary (or the director the Board designates) · **Checker:** Board Chair · **When:** Each meeting.

**Steps**

1. **Agenda** sent at least 7 days before, with papers. Standing items: compliance dashboard (SGO-001 §8), financial report, 90% schedule (quarterly), conflict disclosures, Exception Log.
2. **Open with a conflict call.** Chair asks each director to disclose any conflict on agenda items. Record each disclosure.
3. **Conflict items** follow [COI-001 §§6–7](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md): facts, interested person leaves, QD decide, minimum two QD in favor.
4. **Minutes template** (F-17) records attendance, quorum, disclosures, who left the room, votes by name on conflict items, data relied on, and decisions.
5. **Draft minutes** within 14 days; approved at the next meeting; signed and saved.
6. **Action log** tracks every decision's owner and due date.

**Record:** `01-Governance/Minutes/<year>/`.

## SOP-13 Whistleblower Report Intake and Investigation

**Purpose:** Handle every concern fairly, confidentially and on time.
**Owner:** Investigation Lead per [WB-001 §8](04-whistleblower-policy.md) · **Checker:** Qualified directors · **When:** Each report.

**Steps**

1. **Log** the report in the confidential **Report Log** (F-18) the day received (date, channel, category, no reporter name in the log).
2. **Route.** If the report involves the receiver, a Founding Director or a Board majority, hand it to the IRC the same day.
3. **Acknowledge** within 5 business days if the reporter can be contacted; remind them of non-retaliation and their right to report externally.
4. **Assess** within 10 business days: in scope? interim steps (for example, suspend payment authority)? legal hold (SOP-15)?
5. **Investigate**: gather documents, interview, give the subject a chance to respond.
6. **Conclude** within 60 days with written findings and recommended action; explain any extension to QD.
7. **Report** summary to QD; tell the reporter the matter was addressed where appropriate.
8. **Close** in F-18 with outcome and date.

**Record:** `08-Restricted/Whistleblower/`.

## SOP-14 Privacy and Security Incident Response

**Purpose:** Limit harm and meet notice deadlines.
**Owner:** Privacy Officer · **Checker:** Counsel · **When:** Any suspected incident (lost device, wrong email recipient, account takeover, vendor breach).

**Steps**

1. **Report immediately** to the PO (or a disinterested director if PO is involved). Start the **Incident Record** (F-19) with discovery date and time.
2. **Contain within 24 hours:** reset passwords, revoke access, remote-wipe devices, recall emails, notify the vendor.
3. **Assess within 5 business days:** what data, how many people, which states, whether encrypted.
4. **Decide on notice with counsel.** For Tennessee residents, notice is generally required within **45 days** of discovery for unencrypted personal information; consumer reporting agencies if more than 1,000 people. Note other states' rules.
5. **Notify** individuals, schools, the IRS or TDOE as required, using counsel-approved letters.
6. **Report** to the Board; record lessons learned and fixes.

**Record:** `08-Restricted/Privacy-Incidents/`.

## SOP-15 Records Filing, Legal Holds and Annual Destruction

**Purpose:** Keep what must be kept, find it fast, and destroy the rest safely.
**Owner:** Records Custodian · **Checker:** A second officer · **When:** Ongoing filing; legal holds as needed; destruction each **February**.

**Steps**

1. **File** every record in the standard folder (§1.2) using the naming format `YYYY-MM-DD_Type_Description` (for example, `2027-01-28_Ack_Batch1.pdf`).
2. **Platform exports.** Export a full copy of platform records at least annually (each **January**) and before any vendor contract ends.
3. **Legal hold.** When litigation, an audit or an investigation is pending or reasonably anticipated, RC or counsel sends a written hold notice the same day, suspends auto-deletion, and logs who received it.
4. **Annual destruction review (February).** RC lists records past retention under [REC-001 §4](03-records-retention-policy.md) and not under hold; a second officer approves; destroy by cross-cut shredding or secure deletion; record in the **Destruction Log**.
5. **Access review** twice a year with the PO.

**Record:** `01-Governance/Records/` (index, hold notices, destruction log).

## SOP-17 Approval of Credit-Related Communications

**Purpose:** Every public statement about the tax credit is accurate and consistent.
**Owner:** CD · **Checker:** ED · **When:** Before publishing any new or changed website text, flyer, email, video or social post that mentions the credit.

**Steps**

1. Drafter submits the item with the **Communications Checklist** (F-21):
   - credit is nonrefundable, $1,700 per taxpayer cap (up to $3,400 joint if each spouse gives), reduced by state credits, limited to tax liability, 5-year carryforward;
   - only cash gifts from individuals, **designated** as qualified when given;
   - current status of TDOE certification and IRS listing;
   - no "free," "zero cost" or "not a donation" without the required qualifier;
   - "TENNISSA does not provide tax advice. Please consult your tax professional.";
   - year-end deadline (December 31) where relevant;
   - no student or family named or pictured without written consent.
2. CD approves or returns with edits within 5 business days.
3. Save the approved version with its approval date.

**Record:** `02-Compliance-25F/Communications/`.

## SOP-18 Onboarding and Offboarding People

**Purpose:** Everyone starts with the right training and access, and loses access promptly when they leave.
**Owner:** ED · **Checker:** CD · **When:** Each new or departing director, officer, employee, committee member, reviewer, volunteer or contractor.

**Onboarding (before any access to data or money)**

1. Signed Code of Ethics acknowledgment ([ETH-001 §8](13-code-of-ethics.md)).
2. Signed confidentiality agreement ([PRIV-001 §12](06-privacy-confidentiality-policy.md)).
3. Signed COI/disqualified-person statement; update the register (SOP-03).
4. Privacy and security training completed.
5. Employees: job description with exempt/non-exempt status, Form I-9, payroll setup.
6. Accounts created with MFA and the least access needed; logged on the **Access List** (F-22).

**Offboarding**

1. Remove all system and bank access within **2 business days**; update F-22.
2. Collect TENNISSA records and devices; confirm deletion of copies on personal devices in writing.
3. Update bank signers and the register if needed.

**Record:** `07-People/` and `01-Governance/Registers/`.

## SOP-19 Policy and SOP Change Control

**Purpose:** Keep one current, approved version of every policy and SOP.
**Owner:** CD · **Checker:** Board Chair · **When:** As needed; full review each **October** before the new award year.

**Steps**

1. Anyone may propose a change on the **Change Request** (F-23), citing the reason (law change, audit finding, error, improvement).
2. CD drafts the change and checks every cross-reference in other policies and SOPs.
3. Counsel or CPA reviews any legal or tax change.
4. **Policies** change only by Board resolution. **SOPs** may be changed by the ED and CD for operational detail, reported to the Board at the next meeting; any SOP change that alters a control, approval or deadline needs Board approval.
5. Update the version number and Revision History (1.1 minor, 2.0 substantive); save the prior version permanently.
6. Notify everyone affected and update training within 30 days.

**Record:** `01-Governance/Policies/` and `01-Governance/SOPs/`, each with all prior versions.
