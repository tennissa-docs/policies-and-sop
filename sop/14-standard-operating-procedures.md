> **Plain-language summary.** The policies say *what* TENNISSA must do. The SOPs (one file each, listed in §2) say *how*, step by step, who does each step, who checks it, and what record proves it was done. If everyone follows the same steps, TENNISSA gets the same result every time, and an auditor can see it.

---

## 1. How to Use This Manual

1. **Policies control.** If an SOP conflicts with a policy, the policy wins and the SOP owner fixes the SOP.
2. **Every SOP has the same parts:** purpose, owner, checker, when it runs, steps, controls, and the record that proves completion.
3. **Maker and checker.** Wherever possible, the person who does a task (**Maker**) is not the person who reviews it (**Checker**). With a small team, a disinterested director or outside bookkeeper may serve as Checker.
4. **No record, not done.** A step counts as complete only when its record is saved in the location named in the SOP.
5. **Exceptions.** Any departure from an SOP is approved in advance by the SOP owner (or, for the owner's own tasks, by another officer), written in the **Exception Log** (Form F-20), and reported to the Board at the next meeting.

### 1.2 Standard record locations

TENNISSA keeps one shared, access-controlled drive with this folder structure. Every SOP saves to one of these folders. Year folders use the calendar year.

```
TENNISSA/
  01-Governance/        Board minutes, resolutions, policies, SOPs, registers
  02-Compliance-25F/    TDOE, IRS SGO portal, 90% schedules, certifications, audits
  03-Donors/            Gift log, designations, acknowledgments, IRS reports
  04-Scholarships/      Applications (platform export), award files, appeals
  05-Schools/           Verification files, participation agreements
  06-Finance/           Bank statements, reconciliations, invoices, reimbursements
  07-People/            Personnel, payroll, I-9 (restricted), acknowledgments
  08-Restricted/        Whistleblower files, privacy incidents (IRC/PO access only)
```

Protected information follows [PRIV-001](06-privacy-confidentiality-policy.md). Retention follows [REC-001](03-records-retention-policy.md).
