## Procedure

1. **Publish (before the window opens):** criteria, priority rules, ranking method, award tiers, deadline, required documents, and the privacy notice. Save a dated copy.
2. **Intake** through the approved platform only, with signed consents (data sharing with school; FERPA consent where needed).
3. **Completeness check** within 10 business days of receipt. Send one written request for missing items with a 10-business-day deadline. Incomplete files after the deadline are closed and logged.
4. **Residence and public-school eligibility.** Confirm Tennessee residence (for example, lease, utility bill or state ID) and school age, or a documented exception.
5. **Household income and family size** for the **prior calendar year**, using one method, recorded on the **Eligibility Worksheet** (F-08):
   - **Direct**: tax return or IRS transcript, W-2s, pay stubs, other cash income; compare to the IRS-published income limit for the area and family size; or
   - **Categorical**: benefit award letter dated within 12 months (SNAP, TANF, WIC, Section 8, SSI); or
   - **Foster-child safe harbor**.
   Redact Social Security numbers on upload; do not request them.
6. **Enrollment.** Confirm enrollment or acceptance at a participating school with a current F-06.
7. **Disqualified-person screen** against the register (SOP-03). Record the result and screener's initials.
8. **Other Assistance.** Record all EFS/ESA/IEA, school aid and other scholarships disclosed, with amounts and status (applied, awarded, received). Ask the school to confirm school aid.
9. **Eligibility decision.** Mark eligible / ineligible with reason. Ineligible applicants get a written notice with appeal rights ([SCH-001 §11](01-scholarship-policy.md)).
10. **Quality check.** A Scholarship Committee member re-verifies a random 10% of eligible files (minimum 5) before ranking. Any error triggers review of all files by that verifier.

## Controls

A verifier with a relationship to an applicant does not touch that file. Same documents, same method, same deadline for everyone.

## Records

Platform file plus F-08; export to `04-Scholarships/<year>/`.
