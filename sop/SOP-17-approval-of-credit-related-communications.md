## Procedure

1. Drafter submits the item with the **Communications Checklist** (F-21):
   - credit is nonrefundable, $1,700 per taxpayer cap (up to $3,400 joint if each spouse gives), reduced by state credits, limited to tax liability, 5-year carryforward;
   - only cash gifts from individuals, **designated** as qualified when given;
   - current status of TDOE certification and IRS listing;
   - no "free," "zero cost" or "not a donation" without the required qualifier;
   - "TENNISSA does not provide tax advice. Please consult your tax professional.";
   - year-end deadline (December 31) where relevant;
   - no student or family named or pictured without written consent.
2. CD approves or returns with edits within 5 business days.
3. Save the approved version with its approval date.
