Role codes (ED, FCD, CD, BC, QD, SC, RC, PO, IRC) are defined in the [SOP Manual §1.1](14-standard-operating-procedures.md#11-roles-used-in-this-manual). If this SOP conflicts with a policy, the policy controls.

---

## Procedure

1. **Enrollment confirmation.** Before each term, the school confirms in writing (F-10) which recipients are enrolled, their current charges and any change in school aid.
2. **Prepare the payment batch** from approved F-09 amounts for the term, one line per student, totaled by school.
3. **Dual authorization.** FCD prepares; a second authorized signer approves in the bank system. Payments go only to school bank details verified under SOP-04.
4. **Send remittance** to each school listing students and amounts (no other personal data).
5. **School confirmation.** School confirms amounts were applied to student accounts within 15 business days.
6. **Withdrawals and overpayments.** When a student withdraws, changes school or Other Assistance increases, request a refund in writing within 5 business days. Track on the **Refund Log** (F-11).
7. **Deposit refunds** in the Section 25F account. Record the refund year (it is new income for SOP-09).
8. **Follow up** unpaid refunds at 30 and 60 days; escalate to ED at 60 days.

## Controls

No payment to a parent or student. No payment without F-10 for that term.

## Records

`06-Finance/<year>/Scholarship-Payments/`, F-10, F-11.
