## Procedure

1. **File** every record in the standard folder ([SOP Manual §1.2](14-standard-operating-procedures.md#12-standard-record-locations)) using the naming format `YYYY-MM-DD_Type_Description` (for example, `2027-01-28_Ack_Batch1.pdf`).
2. **Platform exports.** Export a full copy of platform records at least annually (each **January**) and before any vendor contract ends.
3. **Legal hold.** When litigation, an audit or an investigation is pending or reasonably anticipated, RC or counsel sends a written hold notice the same day, suspends auto-deletion, and logs who received it.
4. **Annual destruction review (February).** RC lists records past retention under [REC-001 §4](03-records-retention-policy.md) and not under hold; a second officer approves; destroy by cross-cut shredding or secure deletion; record in the **Destruction Log**.
5. **Access review** twice a year with the PO.
