## Procedure

1. **Agenda** sent at least 7 days before, with papers. Standing items: compliance dashboard (SGO-001 §8), financial report, 90% schedule (quarterly), conflict disclosures, Exception Log.
2. **Open with a conflict call.** Chair asks each director to disclose any conflict on agenda items. Record each disclosure.
3. **Conflict items** follow [COI-001 §§6–7](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md): facts, interested person leaves, QD decide, minimum two QD in favor.
4. **Minutes template** (F-17) records attendance, quorum, disclosures, who left the room, votes by name on conflict items, data relied on, and decisions.
5. **Draft minutes** within 14 days; approved at the next meeting; signed and saved.
6. **Action log** tracks every decision's owner and due date.
