## Procedure

### Onboarding (before any access to data or money)

1. Signed Code of Ethics acknowledgment ([ETH-001 §8](13-code-of-ethics.md)).
2. Signed confidentiality agreement ([PRIV-001 §12](06-privacy-confidentiality-policy.md)).
3. Signed COI/disqualified-person statement; update the register (SOP-03).
4. Privacy and security training completed.
5. Employees: job description with exempt/non-exempt status, Form I-9, payroll setup.
6. Accounts created with MFA and the least access needed; logged on the **Access List** (F-22).

### Offboarding

1. Remove all system and bank access within **2 business days**; update F-22.
2. Collect TENNISSA records and devices; confirm deletion of copies on personal devices in writing.
3. Update bank signers and the register if needed.
