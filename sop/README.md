# TENNISSA Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

**TENNISSA** · SOP set Version 1.0 · Draft for Board review, November 2026

This folder holds TENNISSA's 19 Standard Operating Procedures. The [policies](../README.md) say **what** TENNISSA must do; the SOPs say **how**: the exact steps, who does them, who checks them, when, and what record proves they were done. Following the same SOP every time gives TENNISSA consistent results and a clear audit trail.

> **Start here.** The shared rules for all SOPs (role codes, Maker/Checker, the Exception Log, the shared-drive folder structure), the master compliance calendar, the forms list and the rollout plan are in the [SOP Manual (SOP-001)](../14-standard-operating-procedures.md). Read it once before using any SOP.

### Most critical at launch

These five protect donors' tax credits and TENNISSA's place on Tennessee's SGO list. Put them in place before credit-eligible giving begins (January 1, 2027) or before the first award cycle:

1. **SOP-01** — every gift classified and deposited in the right account.
2. **SOP-02** — acknowledgments with unique donor numbers by January 31; IRS report by February 28.
3. **SOP-03** — no scholarship to a disqualified person.
4. **SOP-06** — every award calculated the same way under the last-dollar model.
5. **SOP-09** — 90% of each year's income paid as scholarships by the end of the following year.

## How Each SOP Is Laid Out

Every SOP file uses the same structure, so anyone can pick up any SOP and follow it:

1. **Header table** — SOP ID, version, status, effective date, owner, checker, frequency, policy basis.
2. **Purpose** — one sentence on why the SOP exists.
3. **Procedure** — numbered steps, in order.
4. **Controls** — the checks that must happen and what triggers escalation.
5. **Records** — what is saved and where on the shared drive.
6. **Revision History** — every approved change.

### Ground rules (from the SOP Manual)

- **Policies control.** If an SOP conflicts with a policy, the policy wins and the SOP is corrected.
- **Maker and Checker.** The person doing a task is not the person checking it, wherever possible.
- **No record, not done.** A step is complete only when its record is saved in the named folder.
- **Exceptions** are approved in advance, logged on the Exception Log (Form F-20), and reported to the Board.

## Naming and Adding SOPs

- **File names:** `SOP-NN-short-title-in-lowercase.md` (two-digit number, hyphens, no spaces).
- **IDs:** `TENNISSA-SOP-NN`. Numbers are never reused, even if an SOP is retired.
- **New SOP:** copy the layout of any existing SOP, take the next free number, add it to the tables in this README and to [SOP Manual §2](../14-standard-operating-procedures.md#2-sop-index), and follow [SOP-19](SOP-19-policy-and-sop-change-control.md).

## Version Control and Approval

- All SOPs are **Version 1.0** and take effect when the Board adopts them with the policies.
- Operational detail in an SOP may be updated by the Executive Director and Compliance Director and reported to the Board at its next meeting. Any change to a **control, approval or deadline** needs Board approval ([SOP-19](SOP-19-policy-and-sop-change-control.md)).
- Minor edits become 1.1, 1.2 and so on; substantive revisions become 2.0. Prior versions are kept permanently under [REC-001](../03-records-retention-policy.md).
- All SOPs are reviewed every October, after each annual audit, and whenever a related policy changes.
