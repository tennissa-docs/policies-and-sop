## Procedure

| By | Step | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Jan 15 | Close December books; final reconciliations (SOP-08) | FCD |
| Jan 31 | 90% test for prior year and 85% activity test (SOP-09); donor acknowledgments sent (SOP-02) | FCD / CD |
| Feb 15 | Engage auditor (qualified independent third party, or independent committee if total receipts were $500,000 or less), approved by QD | QD |
| Feb 28 | IRS SGO portal contribution report (SOP-02) | CD |
| Mar 1 | School reverification complete (SOP-04) | ED |
| Apr 1 | Tennessee annual report to Secretary of State | ED |
| Apr 15 | Draft financial statements and audit fieldwork | FCD / auditor |
| Apr 30 | Audit report received; QD meet auditor without management; report sent to TDOE | QD / CD |
| May 1 | Draft Form 990 with Section 25F annual certification and operational information circulated to every director | CPA / FCD |
| May 15 | File Form 990 (or extension); send certification copy to TDOE | FCD |
| Per TN deadline | Renew Tennessee charitable registration or exemption | ED |

## Controls

The Form 990, certification and audit figures must agree with the Gift Log, SOP-02 totals and SOP-09 schedule. CD signs a tie-out sheet before filing.

## Records

`02-Compliance-25F/<year>/` and `06-Finance/<year>/Year-End/`.

> **⚑ Review flag SOP-R1.** Audit and certification deadlines are set conservatively here because the proposed regulations leave timing to IRS guidance. Update this table when the IRS or TDOE publishes deadlines ([SGO-001 SGO-R3](08-section-25f-compliance-policy.md)).
