> **Plain-language summary.** TENNISSA is an equal opportunity employer committed to a safe, respectful workplace. Employment is at-will. Pay is reasonable, fixed in advance and approved by directors without a conflict. Hourly employees record all their time and never work off the clock.

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets basic employment standards for TENNISSA, ensures compliance with federal and Tennessee employment laws, and makes sure compensation, especially for officers and directors, meets the IRS rules for § 501(c)(3) organizations.

## 2. Employment Authority and Oversight

1. **The Board** hires, evaluates, sets the compensation of, and may remove the Executive Director, and approves the compensation of any officer, any director in a paid role, and anyone related to a director or officer (§5).
2. **The Executive Director** hires, supervises and may end the employment of other staff, within the Board-approved budget and positions, and sets their pay within approved ranges.
3. **No one** may hire, supervise, set pay for or evaluate a family member. A director or officer who is related to a candidate or employee discloses it under the [Conflict of Interest Policy](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md).
4. **Job descriptions.** Every position, including any paid officer role, has a written description of duties, reporting line, and exempt or non-exempt status. Duties performed as a paid officer are distinct from unpaid service as a director.

## 3. At-Will Employment

Employment at TENNISSA is at-will. Either the employee or TENNISSA may end the relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice. Only the Board, in a written agreement approved under §5, may change this for any position. Nothing in this policy creates a contract of employment.

## 4. Equal Employment Opportunity

TENNISSA provides equal employment opportunity to all employees and applicants without regard to race, color, national origin, sex (including pregnancy), age, disability, genetic information, veteran or military status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable federal or Tennessee law. TENNISSA provides reasonable accommodations for disability and for pregnancy-related limitations as the law requires.

**Religious organization.** As a faith-based organization, TENNISSA may make employment decisions based on religion where the law permits, including under the religious-organization exemption in Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. § 2000e-1(a)) and its Articles of Incorporation. This exemption concerns religion only; it does not permit discrimination on any other protected basis.

> **⚑ Review flag EMP-R1.** Employment counsel should confirm (a) whether TENNISSA qualifies as a "religious corporation" for the Title VII exemption, and (b) which federal and Tennessee laws apply at TENNISSA's current headcount (for example, Title VII and the ADA apply at 15 employees, the Tennessee Human Rights Act at 8, and Tennessee workers' compensation generally at 5).

## 5. Compensation (Officers, Directors and Staff)

1. **Reasonable.** Compensation is comparable to what similar organizations pay for similar services, considering the duties, hours and qualifications.
2. **Approved in advance by qualified directors.** Compensation of the Executive Director, any officer, any director in a paid role, and anyone related to a director or officer is approved in advance only by qualified (disinterested) directors under [COI-001 §11](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md), with at least two qualified directors voting in favor. The person concerned does not participate.
3. **Documented.** The approval records the comparability data relied on, the terms, and the vote, at the time of approval, to qualify for the rebuttable presumption of reasonableness under Treas. Reg. § 53.4958-6.
4. **Fixed.** Pay is a fixed salary or hourly rate for a stated period. It is **never** a percentage of, or formula based on, contributions, scholarships or revenue. Bonuses, if any, are approved in advance on the same basis and are not tied to fundraising totals.
5. **No private inurement.** No part of TENNISSA's net earnings may benefit any insider. Excess compensation can trigger excise taxes under IRC § 4958 on the person and on approving managers, and can endanger tax-exempt status.
6. **Operating account only.** All pay comes from the operating account through payroll, with required withholding and reporting, never from the Section 25F account.
7. **Directors.** Directors receive no pay for serving as directors.
8. **Disclosure.** Compensation of officers, directors and key employees is reported on Form 990 as required.

> **⚑ Review flag EMP-R2.** Until independent directors are seated, there may be fewer than two qualified directors to approve founder compensation ([COI-001 §8](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md)). No founder compensation should be paid until it is approved through a lawful process.

## 6. Classification and Payroll

1. Each position is classified as exempt or non-exempt under the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) based on its duties and pay, as documented in the job description.
2. People who perform services under TENNISSA's direction and control, including paid officers, are paid as **employees** through payroll, not as independent contractors, unless counsel advises otherwise in writing.
3. TENNISSA completes Form I-9 for every employee and meets federal and Tennessee payroll, unemployment insurance and new-hire reporting requirements.

## 7. Timekeeping for Non-Exempt Employees

All non-exempt employees, including any Founding Director serving as a paid non-exempt officer, must comply with the FLSA:

1. **Time tracking.** Record all hours worked each day, accurately.
2. **Hours limits.** As set in Resolution 2026-001, officers are limited to **130 hours per month** and **40 hours per workweek**. Overtime requires prior written Board approval and, if worked, is paid as the law requires.
3. **No off-the-clock work.** No one may perform paid duties without recording the time. Doing one's paid job "as a volunteer" off the clock is prohibited. Unpaid service as a director (for example, attending Board meetings) is not counted as work time.

> **⚑ Review flag EMP-R3.** Resolution 2026-001 was not included in this review. Counsel should confirm that its terms (positions, classification, hours caps and approvals) match this policy and [COI-001](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md).

## 8. Performance Oversight

1. The Board (acting through its qualified directors) evaluates the Executive Director in writing at least once a year, before any change in compensation.
2. The Executive Director evaluates other staff in writing at least once a year.
3. Concerns about a paid officer who is also a Founding Director are handled by the qualified directors, with independent advice where needed.

## 9. Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

TENNISSA prohibits harassment and discrimination of any kind.

- **Sexual harassment** includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature that affects employment or creates a hostile work environment.
- **Other harassment** includes slurs, jokes, intimidation or physical violence based on any protected characteristic.
- **Reporting.** Employees who experience or witness harassment report it to the Executive Director or the Board Chair, or, if either is involved, to the Independent Reporting Contact under the [Whistleblower Policy](04-whistleblower-policy.md). Reports are investigated promptly and as confidentially as possible. Retaliation is prohibited.

## 10. Employee Responsibilities and Conduct

Employees must:

- perform their duties honestly and diligently;
- follow TENNISSA's policies, including the [Code of Ethics](13-code-of-ethics.md), [Conflict of Interest](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md) and [Privacy](06-privacy-confidentiality-policy.md) policies;
- protect student, family and donor information; and
- uphold TENNISSA's guiding principles (Amanah, Adl, Ihsan, Rahmah and Ilm) in all dealings with families, participating schools and the public.

## 11. Review

The Board reviews this policy every year.
