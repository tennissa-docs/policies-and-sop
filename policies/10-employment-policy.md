> **Plain-language summary.** TENNISSA is an equal opportunity employer committed to a safe, respectful workplace free of harassment. All employment is at-will, and hourly employees must strictly record their time and never work off the clock.

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy outlines the fundamental employment standards for TENNISSA, ensuring compliance with federal and Tennessee labor laws, promoting a respectful work environment, and enforcing wage-and-hour rules.

## 2. At-Will Employment
Employment at TENNISSA is "at-will." This means that either the employee or TENNISSA may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice. Nothing in this policy or any other document creates a contract of guaranteed employment.

## 3. Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO)
TENNISSA provides equal employment opportunities to all employees and applicants without regard to race, color, national origin, sex, age, disability, genetics, or veteran status, in accordance with applicable federal and Tennessee laws. 
*Note: As a faith-based organization, TENNISSA may exercise its legal right to make certain employment decisions based on religious alignment, as permitted by Title VII of the Civil Rights Act and its Articles of Incorporation.*

## 4. Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination
TENNISSA strictly prohibits harassment or discrimination of any kind. 
- **Sexual Harassment:** Includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, and other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature that creates a hostile work environment.
- **Other Harassment:** Includes slurs, jokes, intimidation, or physical violence based on any protected characteristic.
- **Reporting:** Employees who experience or witness harassment must report it immediately to the Executive Director or the Board Chair. Reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially under the [Whistleblower Policy](04-whistleblower-policy.md). Retaliation for reporting is strictly prohibited.

## 5. Wage and Hour Compliance (Timekeeping)
All non-exempt employees (including the Founding Directors serving as executive officers) must comply strictly with the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA):
1. **Time Tracking:** Employees must accurately record all hours worked each day.
2. **Hours Cap:** As established in Resolution 2026-001, officers are capped at **130 hours per month** and **40 hours per workweek**. Overtime is not permitted without prior written Board approval.
3. **No Off-the-Clock Work:** Employees may not perform work for TENNISSA without recording the time. "Volunteering" to do one's normal paid job duties off the clock is illegal and prohibited.

## 6. Workplace Conduct
Employees are expected to act professionally, maintain the confidentiality of student and donor records, and uphold TENNISSA's Islamic guiding principles (Amanah, Adl, Ihsan, Rahmah, Ilm) in all interactions with families, schools, and the public.
