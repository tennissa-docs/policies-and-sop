> **Plain-language summary.** Keeping TENNISSA on Tennessee's list, and protecting donors' credits, depends on a specific set of federal requirements. This policy gives each requirement an owner and a deadline, and flags what still depends on rules that are not yet final.

## 1. Purpose and Legal Basis

This policy assigns responsibility for TENNISSA's compliance with:

- **IRC § 25F** (qualified elementary and secondary education scholarships), added by § 70411 of Public Law 119-21 (July 4, 2025), and **IRC § 139K**, which excludes qualifying scholarships from recipients' income. Section 25F applies to taxable years ending after December 31, 2026; credit-eligible contributions begin **January 1, 2027**. The program is also called the Federal Scholarship Tax Credit (FSTC) or Education Freedom Tax Credit;
- **Treasury guidance**: Notice 2025-70; Rev. Proc. 2026-6 (state advance elections); **proposed regulations** §§ 1.25F-1 through 1.25F-5 (REG-117199-25, 91 FR 62818, October 2, 2026); and companion **temporary regulations** (T.D. 10057) on registration, reporting and certification; and
- **Tennessee law**: the Federal Tax Credit Scholarship Act (2026), under which Tennessee elected to participate and TDOE certifies SGOs, submits Tennessee's State SGO list to the Treasury and publishes it.

> **⚑ Review flag SGO-R1 (applies to the whole policy).** The proposed regulations are not final. Comments are due December 1, 2026 and a public hearing is scheduled for December 15, 2026. SGOs may rely on them for qualified contributions made on or after January 1, 2027, **but only if they follow them in their entirety and consistently**. Every number, form and date in this policy must be checked against the final regulations, IRS forms and TDOE procedures before it is relied on. If this policy conflicts with current law, the law controls, and the Compliance Director proposes an update.

## 2. Status Requirements

TENNISSA must maintain all of the following at all times:

| Requirement | Evidence | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| § 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charity (not a private foundation) | IRS determination letter; timely Form 990 filings | Executive Director |
| "Located in" Tennessee: authorized to do business in Tennessee and in compliance with Tennessee's rules for charitable organizations | Secretary of State good standing; charitable registration or exemption | Executive Director |
| Certified by TDOE and included on Tennessee's State SGO list for the calendar year | TDOE certification; State SGO list | Compliance Director |
| Registered in the IRS SGO portal, with disclosure on the IRS SGO list authorized | Portal confirmation; IRS SGO list | Compliance Director |
| Separate Section 25F account | Bank records; [FIN-001 §6](02-financial-controls-policy.md) | Financial Controller Director |

Credit-eligible contributions are accepted **only** while every requirement above is current (see [DON-001 §3.2](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md)). If any requirement lapses, or TENNISSA is removed from a State or IRS SGO list, the Compliance Director tells the Board immediately and TENNISSA stops describing contributions as credit-eligible until the requirement is restored.

> **⚑ Review flag SGO-R2.** Counsel should confirm that TENNISSA holds an IRS determination letter as a public charity. The proposed regulations allow a State to list an organization whose exemption application is pending only under specific conditions.

## 3. Core Operating Requirements

| # | Requirement (Section 25F and proposed regulations) | How TENNISSA complies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keep qualified contributions in a separate account, with complete books for it | [FIN-001 §6](02-financial-controls-policy.md) |
| 2 | Spend at least 90% of income on scholarships for eligible students by the end of the year after the year received | [FIN-001 §6.3](02-financial-controls-policy.md) |
| 3 | (Safe harbor) At least 85% of activities are scholarship-granting activities, so the 90% test is measured at the Section 25F account level | [FIN-001 §6.3](02-financial-controls-policy.md); annual documentation |
| 4 | Scholarships only to eligible students: eligible to enroll in public school; household income ≤ 300% of area median gross income (family-size adjusted) for the prior calendar year; residing in Tennessee | [SCH-001 §5](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 5 | Verify household income and family size by permitted methods | [SCH-001 §5.2](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 6 | Scholarships to 10 or more students who do not all attend the same school | [SCH-001 §6.3](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 7 | Priority for prior-year recipients, then their siblings | [SCH-001 §6.1](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 8 | No earmarking or setting aside of contributions for any particular student | [DON-001 §§4–5](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md) |
| 9 | No scholarships to disqualified persons | [SCH-001 §5.6](01-scholarship-policy.md), [COI-001 §§4–5](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md) |
| 10 | Scholarships only for qualified elementary or secondary education expenses; tuition and fees paid directly to the school; schools return excess or erroneous payments | [SCH-001 §8](01-scholarship-policy.md) |
| 11 | Reasonable procedures to prevent and detect fraud and duplicate awards for the same expense | [SCH-001 §7.3](01-scholarship-policy.md), [FIN-001 §10.3](02-financial-controls-policy.md) |
| 12 | Only individual cash contributions designated at the time of gift are qualified contributions | [DON-001 §3](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md) |
| 13 | Donor acknowledgments, IRS contribution reporting, annual certification and annual audit | §4 below |

| **February 15, 2027 (first year only)** | Deadline for TDOE to submit Tennessee's 2027 State SGO list to the IRS. Confirm TENNISSA's inclusion. | Compliance Director |

| **Ongoing** | Record each qualified contribution and its designation. Deposit it only in the Section 25F account. Update the disqualified-person register for substantial contributors. | Financial Controller Director |
| **Quarterly** | Update the 90% spending schedule for the Board. Reconcile the Section 25F account. | Financial Controller Director |
| **Each award cycle** | Reverify schools. Verify student eligibility and income. Screen for disqualified persons. Apply priority. Check Other Assistance. | Executive Director |
| **December 31** | Last day for contributions counted in the current calendar year (state this clearly in materials). Confirm at least 90% of the **prior** year's income has been paid as scholarships. | Executive Director; Financial Controller Director |
| **January (each year)** | Collect annual conflict and disqualified-person statements ([COI-001 Appendix A](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md)). Determine substantial contributors for the prior year. | Board Chair |
| **By January 31** | Send each donor of qualified contributions the written acknowledgment with TENNISSA's EIN, total designated contributions, unique donor number and goods/services statement, so donors can file **Form 8525**. | Compliance Director |
| **By February 28** | Report each donor's name, address and total qualified contributions for the prior year through the IRS SGO portal. | Compliance Director |
| **After year-end** | Complete the **annual financial and programmatic audit** by a qualified independent third party (or, if total receipts were $500,000 or less, a committee of independent persons unrelated to management, signed under penalties of perjury). Give the report to TDOE. The qualified directors receive it. | Financial Controller Director |
| **By the 15th day of the 5th month after year-end (May 15)** | File Form 990 with the Section 25F **annual certification and operational information** attached, and send a copy to TDOE. | **By the Tennessee deadline** | Renew Tennessee charitable registration or exemption. | Executive Director |

## 5. Corrections and Errors

1. If TENNISSA discovers an error in a donor acknowledgment, an IRS report, a 90% calculation or a scholarship award, the Compliance Director tells the Executive Director and the Board Chair within **5 business days**.
2. With CPA or counsel guidance, TENNISSA issues corrected acknowledgments and amended reports as IRS procedures require, promptly enough that donors can correct their returns.
3. If a scholarship was paid to an ineligible student or a disqualified person, [SCH-001](01-scholarship-policy.md) §12 applies. The Board decides with counsel whether any recovery, correction or disclosure is required, including whether the inadvertent-award relief in proposed Treas. Reg. § 1.25F-3(d)(2)(ii) applies.

## 6. Public Communications

Everything that describes the credit (website, flyers, emails, videos) must:

- state that the credit is **nonrefundable**, capped at **$1,700 per taxpayer** per year (up to $3,400 on a joint return where each spouse contributes, under the proposed regulations), reduced by any state credit for the same gift, and limited to each donor's own tax liability, with unused amounts carried forward up to five years;
- state that only **cash gifts from individuals**, designated as qualified contributions at the time of the gift, can qualify;
- state the **status** of TENNISSA's TDOE certification and IRS listing until both are complete;
- avoid "free," "zero cost" or "this is not a donation" unless the qualifier in [DON-001 §10](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md) appears next to it; and
- include: "TENNISSA does not provide tax advice. Please consult your tax professional."

The Compliance Director approves new credit-related materials before publication.

## 7. Service Providers

TENNISSA may use a third-party scholarship and donation platform. Using a service provider does not shift TENNISSA's legal responsibility. The Compliance Director:

- confirms each year that the platform's acknowledgments and reports match current IRS formats, including unique donor numbers;
- confirms the platform records each donor's qualified-contribution designation and deposits designated gifts at their full amount into the Section 25F account;
- reconciles platform reports to the bank and general ledger before each filing; and
- confirms a data-protection agreement is in place under [PRIV-001 §6](06-privacy-confidentiality-policy.md).

## 8. Reporting to the Board

At every regular meeting, the Compliance Director gives a short compliance dashboard covering:

- the status of each requirement in §2;
- the 90% spending schedule and the 85% activity-test position;
- upcoming calendar items;
- any errors or corrections; and
- regulatory changes.

## 9. Review

The Board reviews this policy every year, and within 60 days after final Section 25F regulations, new IRS procedures or TDOE rules are issued.
