## 1. Purpose
This policy is adopted to comply with IRS guidelines regarding joint ventures and to support TENNISSA's responses on IRS Form 990, Part VI, Line 16a. It ensures that any joint venture or similar arrangement with a taxable entity protects TENNISSA's tax-exempt status and charitable assets.

## 2. Definition of Joint Venture
For this policy, a "Joint Venture" is any joint ownership or contractual arrangement through which there is an agreement to jointly undertake a specific business enterprise, investment, or exempt-purpose activity. This includes partnerships, limited liability companies (LLCs), and revenue-sharing arrangements with for-profit entities.
*Note: Standard vendor contracts (e.g., paying a software provider) or school participation agreements are not considered joint ventures.*

## 3. Required Safeguards
Before entering into any Joint Venture, the Board of Directors must review and approve the arrangement. The Board shall negotiate terms and safeguards that ensure TENNISSA’s exempt status is protected. 

The agreement must explicitly include the following safeguards:
1. **Control over Exempt Purpose:** TENNISSA must retain sufficient control over the venture to ensure that it furthers TENNISSA’s charitable and educational purposes.
2. **Priority of Exempt Purpose:** The venture must prioritize TENNISSA's exempt purposes over maximizing profits for the taxable partner.
3. **No Private Inurement:** The arrangement must not result in private inurement or impermissible private benefit to any individual or for-profit entity.
4. **Arm's Length Negotiation:** All contracts and transactions with the venture must be negotiated at arm's length and reflect fair market value.

## 4. Board Approval and Legal Review
Any proposed Joint Venture must be reviewed by TENNISSA's legal counsel to evaluate tax-exempt compliance before it is presented to the Board. Approval requires a majority vote of disinterested directors.

## 5. Ongoing Monitoring
The Executive Director and the Board shall monitor any active Joint Venture periodically to ensure the taxable partner is complying with the agreed-upon safeguards and that the venture continues to serve TENNISSA's charitable mission.
