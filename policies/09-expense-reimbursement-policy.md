> **Plain-language summary.** If you spend your own money on approved TENNISSA business, you can be paid back, tax-free for employees, if you submit receipts and a business purpose within 60 days and return any unused advance within 120 days. Someone other than you approves it, and it is paid from the operating account.

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes an **accountable plan** under IRC § 62(c) and Treas. Reg. § 1.62-2, so that reimbursements of legitimate business expenses are not treated as wages to employees. It applies to employees, officers, directors and volunteers who incur expenses for TENNISSA.

## 2. The Three Requirements

Every reimbursed expense must meet all three:

1. **Business connection.** The expense is ordinary, necessary and incurred while performing services for TENNISSA, and was authorized in advance where §5 requires.
2. **Substantiation.** The individual reports the date, place, amount and business purpose (and, for meals, the attendees), with itemized receipts, within the time in §3.
3. **Return of excess.** Any advance not spent on substantiated expenses is returned within the time in §3.

## 3. Time Limits (Fixed-Date Safe Harbor)

- **Advances** are made no more than **30 days** before the expected expense.
- **Expense reports** with receipts are submitted within **60 days** after the expense is paid or incurred.
- **Excess advances** are returned within **120 days** after the expense is paid or incurred.

Expenses submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed unless the Financial Controller Director (or, for that officer's own expenses, the Executive Director) approves a written exception. Any late reimbursement to an employee that does not meet the accountable-plan rules is reported as wages.

## 4. Reimbursable and Non-Reimbursable Expenses

**Reimbursable**, if reasonable and within budget:

- **Mileage** for business travel in a personal vehicle, at the IRS standard business mileage rate in effect on the date of travel, with a log of date, destination, purpose and miles. Commuting is not reimbursable.
- **Travel**: economy airfare, reasonable lodging and ground transportation for trips approved in advance.
- **Meals**: reasonable cost while traveling overnight on business, or at a business meeting with documented attendees and purpose.
- **Supplies and services**: office supplies, software, postage and similar items for TENNISSA operations, where a direct purchase by TENNISSA was not practical.

**Not reimbursable**:

- personal expenses, traffic and parking fines, and personal entertainment;
- first-class or business-class airfare;
- extravagant meals or lodging;
- alcohol, consistent with TENNISSA's guiding principles;
- gifts to scholarship applicants, families, participating schools or officials; and
- any expense that would benefit a director, officer or family member personally.

## 5. Approval Authority

1. The individual submits an expense report with itemized receipts.
2. **No one approves their own reimbursement**, or that of a family member.
3. Approvers:

| Person seeking reimbursement | Approver |
|---|---|
| Staff, volunteers and directors (other than below) | Executive Director |
| Executive Director | Financial Controller Director or another disinterested officer |
| Financial Controller Director | Executive Director or another disinterested officer |
| Any Founding Director, where the only available approvers are other Founding Directors | Also reviewed by an independent director (once seated) at the next monthly review under [FIN-001 §10.2](02-financial-controls-policy.md) |

4. **Pre-approval** is required for any single trip or expense over **$500**, and for any expense not in the approved budget (under [FIN-001 §5](02-financial-controls-policy.md)).
5. Reimbursements over $1,000 require two signers under [FIN-001 §4.4](02-financial-controls-policy.md).

## 6. Related-Party Purchases

An individual may not seek reimbursement for goods or services bought from a business owned or controlled by themselves or a family member unless the purchase was disclosed and approved in advance under the [Conflict of Interest Policy](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md).

## 7. Payment and Records

1. Reimbursements are paid **only from the operating account**, by check or direct deposit. No expense is ever paid from the Section 25F account ([FIN-001 §6.2](02-financial-controls-policy.md)).
2. TENNISSA cards, if issued, follow the same documentation rules; card statements are reconciled monthly with receipts.
3. Expense reports and receipts are kept for 7 years under the [Records Retention Policy](03-records-retention-policy.md).

## 8. Volunteers and Directors

Volunteers and unpaid directors are reimbursed under the same rules. Volunteers who choose not to seek reimbursement should consult their own tax adviser about any deduction available to them; TENNISSA does not give tax advice.

> **⚑ Review flag EXP-R1.** The CPA should confirm how reimbursements to unpaid directors and volunteers are reported, and whether any reimbursement to a director who is also an employee must run through payroll.

## 9. Review

The Board reviews this policy every year.
