> **Plain-language summary.** If you spend your own money for TENNISSA, you can be paid back tax-free if you follow this rule: the expense must be for business, you must provide a receipt within 60 days, and you must return any extra money advanced to you within 120 days.

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes an "Accountable Plan" under Internal Revenue Code (IRC) Section 62 and Treasury Regulation § 1.62-2. By following this policy, reimbursements for legitimate business expenses are not treated as taxable income to the employee or volunteer.

## 2. The Three Requirements
To qualify for tax-free reimbursement, all expenses must meet three rules:
1. **Business Connection:** The expense must be ordinary, necessary, and incurred while performing duties for TENNISSA.
2. **Substantiation:** The individual must provide the date, time, place, amount, and business purpose of the expense, supported by original receipts, within a reasonable period.
3. **Return of Excess:** If TENNISSA advances money for an anticipated expense, any unused amount must be returned within a reasonable period.

## 3. Reasonable Periods (Safe Harbors)
- **Advances:** May be made no more than 30 days before the expense is paid or incurred.
- **Substantiation:** Expense reports and receipts must be submitted within **60 days** after the expense is incurred.
- **Return of Excess:** Any excess advance must be returned to TENNISSA within **120 days** after the expense is incurred.

*Expenses submitted after 60 days will generally not be reimbursed, or if approved by the Board as an exception, will be reported as taxable income on the employee's W-2.*

## 4. Reimbursable Expenses
- **Mileage:** Reimbursed at the standard IRS mileage rate for business travel. Commuting from home to a regular workplace is not reimbursable.
- **Travel:** Economy-class airfare, reasonable lodging, and ground transportation for approved business trips.
- **Meals:** Reasonable cost of meals while traveling overnight for business, or when conducting a specific business meeting (attendees and business purpose must be documented).
- **Supplies:** Office supplies, software, or postage purchased for TENNISSA operations.

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
- Personal expenses, parking tickets, traffic fines.
- First-class or business-class airfare.
- Extravagant meals or entertainment.
- Expenses paid from the Section 25F Scholarship Account (all administrative expenses must come from the Operating Account).

## 6. Approval Process
1. The individual submits an expense report with attached receipts.
2. No individual may approve their own reimbursement.
3. The Executive Director's expenses must be approved by the Financial Controller Director or another disinterested officer.
4. Reimbursements are paid from the Operating Account.
