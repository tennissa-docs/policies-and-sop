## Governance Structure

### Relationship to the Bylaws

These policies operate under TENNISSA's Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws. If a policy conflicts with the Articles, the Bylaws or the law, those control, and the policy owner proposes an amendment. The policies rely on these Bylaws features, which counsel should confirm before adoption:

- **Founding Directors.** Three Founding Directors hold permanent seats. One serves as Executive Director.
- **Open seats.** Two Board seats are open. [COI-001 §8](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md) requires at least **two independent directors** to be seated by January 1, 2027, so that conflict matters involving founders (including founder compensation) can be decided by at least two qualified directors, as Tennessee law requires.

| Role | Responsibilities in these policies |
|---|---|
| **Board of Directors** | Adopts and reviews all policies; approves budget, compensation, contracts over limits and joint ventures; receives audits and compliance dashboards |
| **Qualified (disinterested) directors** | Decide every matter in which another director or officer has a conflict, including compensation (minimum two in favor) |
| **Board Chair** | Owns COI-001, WB-001 and ETH-001; receives reports not involving the Chair |
| **Executive Director** | Day-to-day management; scholarship administration; donor relations; Privacy Officer and Records Custodian unless the Board designates others |
| **Financial Controller Director (Treasurer)** | Books, bank accounts, 90% spending schedule, audit coordination, cash management, reimbursements |
| **Compliance Director** | Section 25F status, IRS SGO portal, donor acknowledgments, IRS reporting, approval of credit-related materials |
| **Scholarship Committee** | Approves awards under SCH-001; members are Section 25F disqualified persons |
| **Independent Reporting Contact** | Outside counsel, CPA or other independent professional who receives reports involving founders or a Board majority (WB-001) |

## Terminology

These terms are used the same way in every policy.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Board of Directors** (the Board) | TENNISSA's governing body |
| **Executive Director** | TENNISSA's chief executive |
| **Donor** | An individual or entity that makes a gift to TENNISSA |
| **Qualified contribution** | A cash gift from an individual, designated by the donor at the time of the gift as a Section 25F contribution, made while TENNISSA is listed |
| **Section 25F account** | TENNISSA's segregated bank account that holds only qualified contributions and their earnings |
| **Operating account** | The account for all other revenue and all operating costs |
| **Participating school** | A school meeting every requirement of SCH-001 §4 that has signed a participation agreement |
| **Scholarship applicant** | A parent or guardian applying for a student (or the student, if 18 or older) |
| **Scholarship recipient** | A student awarded a TENNISSA scholarship |
| **Qualified Costs / Other Assistance / Remaining Need** | The terms of the last-dollar model (SCH-001 §3) |
| **Disqualified person** | Directors, officers, selection participants, substantial contributors and their Section 25F family members (SCH-001 §5.6) |
| **Qualified director** | A director with no conflict in the matter at hand (COI-001 §3) |
| **TDOE** | Tennessee Department of Education |
