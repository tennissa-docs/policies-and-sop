## 1. Purpose

This policy protects TENNISSA's assets, supports accurate financial reporting, honors donor restrictions, and keeps TENNISSA in compliance with the requirements for § 501(c)(3) public charities, Section 25F and Tennessee law.

## 2. General Requirements

TENNISSA will:

- use funds only for its charitable purposes;
- keep accurate, complete books under generally accepted accounting principles, or another basis the Board approves on its CPA's advice;
- keep Section 25F contributions in a **separate bank account** (§6);
- follow the approved budget and the approval limits in this policy;
- separate duties so that no one person controls a transaction from start to finish (§10.2);
- prevent unauthorized transactions, fraud, conflicts and misuse; and
- report regularly to the Board.

## 3. Fiscal Year and Budget

1. The fiscal year is the calendar year, which matches the Section 25F reporting cycle. Only the Board may change it, after CPA advice.
2. The Executive Director and the Financial Controller Director prepare an annual budget. The Board approves it **before** the fiscal year begins.
3. The budget shows the Section 25F account and the operating account separately. It covers projected contributions, scholarship spending needed to meet the 90% requirement (§6.3), operating expenses, professional fees, the annual audit, insurance and reserves.
4. Material variances (more than 10% or $2,500 in any line, whichever is greater) are reported at the next Board meeting.

## 4. Bank Accounts and Access

1. All accounts are opened in TENNISSA's legal name and EIN, by Board resolution.
2. Required accounts:
   - **Section 25F Scholarship Account.** Holds only Section 25F (qualified) contributions and the earnings on them, plus scholarship refunds from schools (§6.1).
   - **Operating Account.** Holds all other gifts, grants and revenue and pays all operating costs.
   - Further accounts may be opened only by Board resolution.
3. There are at least two authorized signers. Every signer is a director or officer approved by the Board.
4. **Dual authorization** is required for:
   - every payment or transfer from the Section 25F account;
   - any other payment over $1,000; and
   - any change to bank signers, online banking users, or payee bank details.
5. A change to a payee's bank details (for example, a school's account) is confirmed by phone with a known contact before any payment, to guard against payment fraud.
6. Personal and TENNISSA funds are never mixed. TENNISSA cards may not be used for personal purchases.
7. Bank accounts are reconciled monthly, within 15 days of month-end, by someone who did not initiate or approve the payments. A second person reviews and signs the reconciliation.

## 5. Approval Authority

Subject to the approved budget and any Board resolution:

| Transaction | Who may approve |
|---|---|
| Budgeted operating expense up to $2,500 | Executive Director (payments over $1,000 still need two signers under §4.4) |
| Budgeted operating expense over $2,500 | Executive Director **and** Financial Controller Director (or another officer) |
| Unbudgeted expense over $500 | Board, or two disinterested directors acting under Board delegation, in writing **before** the expense |
| Contracts up to $5,000 and up to 12 months (budgeted) | Executive Director |
| Contracts over $5,000 or over 12 months; any loan, lease, guarantee or line of credit | Board, in advance |
| Scholarship payments | Only for awards approved under the [Scholarship Policy](01-scholarship-policy.md); released by two authorized people |
| Any transaction involving an interested person | Qualified (disinterested) directors only, under the [Conflict of Interest Policy](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md) |
| Compensation of any officer or director, or a family member | Qualified directors only, under §11 |

Splitting a transaction to avoid an approval limit is prohibited. No one may approve their own compensation, reimbursement or any payment in which they or a family member have a financial interest.

**No loans to insiders.** TENNISSA will not lend money to, or guarantee a debt of, any director, officer or employee.

## 6. Section 25F Scholarship Account

### 6.1 What goes in

The Section 25F account receives **only**:

- cash contributions from individuals that the donor designated, at the time of the gift, as Section 25F qualified contributions (see [DON-001](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md) §3);
- interest or other earnings on those funds; and
- refunds from schools of scholarship payments made from the account.

Every contribution the donor designates as a qualified contribution is deposited in this account, whether or not the donor ultimately claims a credit. All other gifts go to the operating account.

**Donor-Paid Payment Processing Fees** Donors shall be responsible for any applicable credit card, electronic payment, or platform processing fees associated with their contributions. Such fees shall be separately identified and paid in addition to the intended charitable contribution. Only the amount designated and received by TENNISSA as a charitable contribution shall be recorded as the donor's contribution and considered for Section 25F qualification, subject to applicable law and regulations. Processing fees paid directly to third-party payment processors shall not be represented as qualified contributions or included in TENNISSA's scholarship contribution records.

### 6.2 What goes out

Funds leave the Section 25F account **only** for scholarship payments for eligible students under the [Scholarship Policy](01-scholarship-policy.md). Administrative, fundraising and personnel costs, including all compensation, are paid **only** from the operating account.

### 6.3 The 90% spending requirement

1. **What counts as income.** Under the proposed regulations, an SGO's "income" is its **total gross receipts from all sources**, unless at least 85% of its activities are scholarship-granting activities. An SGO meeting that 85% test may measure income at the Section 25F account level: the qualified contributions received and earnings credited to the account during the year. TENNISSA intends to operate as a scholarship-granting organization and to qualify for that safe harbor. The Financial Controller Director documents the basis for this each year.
2. **Deadline.** At least 90% of each year's income must be **paid** as scholarships for eligible students by the **last day of the following taxable year** (for example, income received in 2027 must be 90% spent by December 31, 2028).
3. **Ordering.** Payments count against the earliest year's income first (first-in, first-out). A scholarship counts as spent only when paid. Amounts paid count toward only one year. A school refund is income in the year it is returned.
4. **Quarterly schedule.** Each quarter, the Financial Controller Director prepares a schedule showing, for each receipt year: income received, scholarships paid against it, the remaining amount needed to reach 90%, and the deadline. The schedule goes to the Board.
5. **Early action.** If a deadline is at risk, the Board acts at least six months before it, for example by raising award tiers or opening a supplemental award cycle.

### 6.4 Holding funds

Section 25F funds are held only as permitted by the [Investment Policy](11-investment-policy.md). Speculative investment is prohibited.

## 7. Receiving Contributions

1. Every contribution is recorded with:
   - date received and amount;
   - method of payment;
   - donor name and address;
   - whether the donor designated it as a Section 25F qualified contribution, and the evidence of that designation;
   - any restriction; and
   - any goods or services provided in return.
2. Where staffing allows, checks are opened and logged by someone other than the person who records them in the books.
3. Online gifts go through the approved donation platform and are reconciled to bank deposits monthly.
4. Acknowledgments and Section 25F written acknowledgments are issued under the [Donor Acceptance Policy](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md) and [SGO-001](08-section-25f-compliance-policy.md). TENNISSA never promises a tax result.

## 8. Payments and Reimbursements

1. Every payment needs supporting documents, such as an invoice, receipt, contract or approved award.
2. Reimbursements follow the [Expense Reimbursement Policy](09-expense-reimbursement-policy.md).
3. Scholarship payments are released only after the school confirms the student's enrollment for the term.
4. Payments to a vendor, school or person related to a director or officer require prior approval under the [Conflict of Interest Policy](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md).

## 9. Financial Reporting

| Report | Prepared by | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly statements of financial position and activities, by account, plus bank reconciliations | Financial Controller Director | 15 days after month-end |
| Quarterly report: contributions, awards, Section 25F account balance, 90% spending schedule, budget variances | Executive Director and Financial Controller Director | Next Board meeting |
| Annual financial statements | Financial Controller Director, with outside CPA | Within 120 days after year-end |
| Annual Section 25F financial and programmatic audit (§10.1) | Qualified independent third party | Per [SGO-001](08-section-25f-compliance-policy.md) calendar |
| Form 990 (or 990-EZ/990-N), with the Section 25F annual certification and operational information | Outside CPA; reviewed by the Board before filing | 15th day of the 5th month after year-end (May 15), plus extensions |
| Tennessee charitable registration renewal (or exemption) and annual report | Executive Director | Per Secretary of State deadlines |

The full Form 990 is provided to every director before it is filed. Suspected fraud, material errors, misuse of funds or serious financial concerns are reported **immediately** to the Board Chair, or to a disinterested director if the Chair is involved, and handled under the [Whistleblower Policy](04-whistleblower-policy.md).

## 10. Audit and Internal Controls

1. **Annual audit.** Each year TENNISSA commissions the financial and programmatic audit required by Section 25F, performed by a qualified independent third party (for example, an independent CPA firm). Under the proposed regulations, an SGO whose total receipts for the year are $500,000 or less may instead use a committee of independent persons unrelated to management, with the report signed under penalties of perjury. The auditor has no relationship with any director or officer. The qualified directors (or an Audit Committee of them) engage the auditor, receive the report and meet with the auditor without management present. The report is provided to TDOE as required.
2. **Separation of duties.** Initiating, approving and recording payments, and reconciling the bank, are done by different people wherever possible. With a small staff, a disinterested director (or an outside bookkeeper engaged by the Board) reviews bank activity and reconciliations at least monthly and documents the review.
3. **Fraud and duplication controls.** TENNISSA maintains procedures to prevent and detect fraud and duplicate scholarship funding, including the Other Assistance check in the [Scholarship Policy](01-scholarship-policy.md) §7.3.
4. **Insurance.** TENNISSA maintains directors and officers liability insurance and crime/fidelity coverage. Crime coverage is at least equal to the highest expected Section 25F account balance, or another amount the Board sets on its broker's advice.

## 11. Compensation

1. Compensation of the Executive Director, any officer, any director serving in a paid role, or anyone related to a director must be:
   - **reasonable**, meaning comparable to what similar organizations pay for similar services;
   - approved **in advance** by qualified directors under [COI-001](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md) §11;
   - supported by **comparability data** and documented **at the time** of approval, to qualify for the rebuttable presumption of reasonableness under Treas. Reg. § 53.4958-6; and
   - a **fixed** salary, hourly rate or fee set in advance, never a percentage of, or formula based on, contributions, scholarships or revenue.
2. Compensation is paid **only from the operating account**, through payroll with required tax withholding and reporting.
3. No compensation is paid automatically, retroactively or before approval.
4. Directors are not paid for serving as directors. They may be reimbursed for reasonable documented expenses.

## 12. Records

Financial records are kept under the [Records Retention Policy](03-records-retention-policy.md).

## 13. Review

The Board reviews this policy every year and whenever federal regulations, IRS guidance, TDOE rules or Tennessee's state scholarship programs change.
