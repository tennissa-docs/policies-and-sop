> **Plain-language summary.** We collect only what we need, keep it in secure systems, share it only when necessary, never sell it, and tell people promptly if something goes wrong.

## 1. Purpose

This policy protects personal and confidential information about scholarship applicants, students, families, donors, participating schools, directors, staff and volunteers, and TENNISSA's own confidential records.

## 2. Protected Information

Protected information includes:

- **Student and family information**: identity, contact details, enrollment, household members, residence and family size;
- **Financial eligibility information**: household income documents, tax returns and transcripts, benefit-program award letters, and scholarship applications;
- **Sensitive student information**: education records obtained from schools, disability-related information, foster-care status, and EFS/ESA/IEA participation and award information;
- **Donor information**: identity, contact details, contribution amounts and designations, unique donor numbers, and tax documents;
- **Payment information**: bank account, card and payment details;
- **Personnel and governance information**: compensation records, personnel files, conflict disclosures, whistleblower reports; and
- **Confidential organizational information**: passwords, credentials, security settings, legal advice, and Board deliberations designated confidential.

Information about students, foster-care status, disability, income documents and identifiers is **highly sensitive** and receives the strongest safeguards in §5.

## 3. Collection and Use

1. **Minimum necessary.** TENNISSA collects only what it needs to administer scholarships, meet Section 25F and other legal obligations, process gifts and operate the organization.
2. **No unnecessary Social Security numbers.** TENNISSA does not collect full Social Security numbers unless the law requires them. It uses redacted documents and transcripts obtained with consent. Section 25F donor acknowledgments use the unique donor number, not a donor's taxpayer identification number.
3. **Purpose limitation.** Information is used only for the purpose for which it was collected. Other Assistance information is used only to calculate awards under [SCH-001](01-scholarship-policy.md) §7.
4. **Notice.** Application, donation and website forms link to a public privacy notice explaining what is collected, why, who receives it (participating schools, service providers, auditors, the IRS and TDOE as required) and how long it is kept.
5. **Children.** TENNISSA's website and online forms are directed to adults. Applications are submitted by a parent or guardian, or by a student who is 18 or older.

## 4. Student Education Records

Participating schools that receive federal funds may be subject to the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). TENNISSA does not ask a school to disclose a student's education records without **signed written consent from the parent** (or an eligible student aged 18 or older). That consent is collected with the application and describes the records to be shared and why. Participation agreements require schools to share only what is necessary, such as enrollment confirmation, tuition charges and school-provided aid.

## 5. Access and Security

1. **Role-based access.** Access is limited to people with a need to know. Scholarship reviewers see only the applications assigned to them, and see income documents only where their role requires it.
2. **Required safeguards:**
   - multi-factor authentication on email, the scholarship and donation platforms, banking and cloud storage;
   - unique accounts, with no shared logins;
   - encryption of laptops and phones used for TENNISSA work, and of files in transit;
   - automatic screen locking and current software updates;
   - a password manager for organizational credentials; and
   - removal of access within **2 business days** after a person's role ends.
3. Protected information may not be stored in personal email or personal cloud accounts, or sent as an unencrypted email attachment when a secure alternative is available.
4. The website interest form collects only contact information and interest type. It may not be used to collect income documents or student records.
5. The Privacy Officer reviews user access to each system at least twice a year.

## 6. Service Providers

Service providers that store or process protected information (for example, scholarship and donation platforms, payment processors, form services, accountants and auditors) must:

- be approved by the Executive Director after a review of their security practices;
- sign a written agreement requiring confidentiality, appropriate security, use only for TENNISSA's purposes, breach notice to TENNISSA within a stated time, data export on request, and return or deletion of data at the end of the contract; and
- keep data within the United States unless the Board approves otherwise.

Using a service provider does not shift TENNISSA's responsibility for the information.

## 7. Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be shared only:

- with participating schools, to confirm enrollment, Qualified Costs and Other Assistance, and to apply awards;
- with service providers under §6;
- with auditors, counsel, the IRS, TDOE or other agencies as the law requires; or
- with the consent of the person concerned.

TENNISSA **never sells, rents or trades** personal information. Names, photographs or stories of students or donors are used publicly only with written consent, given by a parent for minors.

## 8. Donor Information

Donor identities, amounts and unique donor numbers are confidential. Donor names are excluded from public copies of Form 990 (Schedule B). A donor is listed publicly, for example on a donor wall, only after opting in. Donors are never told which students their gifts funded.

## 9. Confidential Organizational Information

Board deliberations designated confidential, legal advice, investigation files, personnel matters, credentials and security settings are confidential. Directors, officers, staff and volunteers may not disclose them outside TENNISSA except as authorized by the Board or required by law. Nothing in this policy limits anyone's right to report to a government agency under the [Whistleblower Policy](04-whistleblower-policy.md).

## 10. Retention and Disposal

Information is kept and destroyed under the [Records Retention Policy](03-records-retention-policy.md).

## 11. Security Incidents and Breach Notification

1. Anyone who suspects loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure or alteration of protected information reports it **immediately** to the Privacy Officer, or to a disinterested director if the Privacy Officer is involved.
2. The Privacy Officer, with counsel:
   - **contains** the incident (for example, resetting credentials, revoking access or notifying service providers);
   - **assesses** what data and which people are affected;
   - **notifies** affected individuals as the law requires. Tennessee's breach-notification law, Tenn. Code Ann. § 47-18-2107, generally requires notice to affected Tennessee residents within **45 days** after discovery of a breach of unencrypted personal information, and notice to consumer reporting agencies when more than 1,000 people are notified. Other states' laws apply to their residents. TENNISSA also notifies participating schools, the IRS or TDOE where required or appropriate;
   - **documents** the incident, response and lessons learned; and
   - **reports** to the Board.
3. Service providers must notify TENNISSA of any incident affecting TENNISSA data within the time stated in their contract.

> **⚑ Review flag PRIV-R1.** Counsel should confirm (a) the current text of Tenn. Code Ann. § 47-18-2107, and (b) that TENNISSA, as a nonprofit, is not subject to the Tennessee Information Protection Act (Tenn. Code Ann. § 47-18-3201 et seq.) or other comprehensive state privacy laws for donors in other states.

## 12. Confidentiality Agreements and Training

1. Directors, officers, staff, volunteers, scholarship reviewers and contractors sign a confidentiality agreement and must protect information during and after their service.
2. Everyone with access to protected information completes privacy and security training when they start and every year after that.
3. Violations may lead to loss of access, removal from a role or other corrective action.

## 13. Individual Requests

Parents, scholarship applicants and donors may ask to see or correct the information TENNISSA holds about them, or ask how it is used. TENNISSA responds within 30 days, subject to legal retention duties and the privacy of others.

## 14. Review

The Board reviews this policy every year, after any significant incident and whenever applicable law changes.
