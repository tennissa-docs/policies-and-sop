## 1. Purpose

This policy sets rules for managing and holding TENNISSA's financial assets. Section 25F contributions must be spent on scholarships within a set time ([FIN-001 §6.3](02-financial-controls-policy.md)), so safety and liquidity come before return. The policy applies to all TENNISSA funds.

## 2. Standard of Care

Those responsible for TENNISSA's funds act in good faith and with the care an ordinarily prudent person in a like position would exercise under similar circumstances, considering TENNISSA's charitable purposes, its spending needs and the need to preserve capital.

## 3. Objectives (in order)

1. **Preservation of capital.** Protect the principal of all funds.
2. **Liquidity.** Keep funds available to meet scholarship payment schedules and operating expenses without penalty.
3. **Faith-based alignment.** Where it is consistent with 1 and 2 and with fiduciary duties, prefer banking and cash-management options consistent with Islamic financial principles (for example, non-interest or permissible profit-sharing accounts), provided they offer equivalent safety, including deposit insurance.
4. **Yield.** A reasonable return is pursued only after the objectives above are met.

## 4. Permitted Holdings

Funds may be held only in:

- checking and savings accounts at banks insured by the FDIC (or credit unions insured by the NCUA);
- insured certificates of deposit whose maturities match scheduled scholarship or operating needs;
- insured money market deposit accounts; and
- U.S. Treasury bills with maturities of one year or less, if the Board approves them by resolution after considering §3.3.

The Board may approve, by resolution, a Sharia-compliant deposit product that meets the safety and liquidity tests above.

## 5. Prohibited Investments

TENNISSA does **not** hold:

- equities (stocks) or funds that hold equities;
- corporate, municipal or high-yield bonds, or bond funds;
- cryptocurrency or other digital assets;
- real estate, commodities, derivatives or private investments; or
- any speculative or illiquid asset.

Noncash gifts of prohibited assets are sold promptly under [DON-001 §8](05-donor-acceptance-policy.md).

## 6. Risk Management

1. **Deposit insurance limits.** Balances at any one bank should not exceed the deposit insurance limit for TENNISSA's ownership category. Where balances are larger, TENNISSA uses more than one bank, a reciprocal-deposit or insured cash sweep program, or Treasury bills approved under §4.
2. **Matching cash to needs.** The Financial Controller Director keeps a 12-month cash forecast showing scheduled scholarship payments by term and operating needs, and holds at least the next term's scheduled scholarship payments in immediately available accounts.
3. **Operating reserve.** The Board sets a target operating reserve (for example, three to six months of operating expenses) to be held in the operating account.
4. **No pledging.** TENNISSA funds may not be pledged as collateral.

## 7. Section 25F Account

1. Section 25F funds are held only in the account required by [FIN-001 §6](02-financial-controls-policy.md) and only in permitted holdings.
2. Earnings on Section 25F funds stay in that account, count as income for the 90% spending requirement, and are used only for scholarships.
3. A non-interest account for Section 25F funds is acceptable and simplifies the 90% calculation.

## 8. Investment Authority

1. The **Board** approves this policy, the banks used, any Treasury bill or Sharia-compliant product under §4, and any exception.
2. The **Financial Controller Director** manages cash within this policy, moves funds between permitted holdings in TENNISSA's name, and may not open new accounts without a Board resolution ([FIN-001 §4.1](02-financial-controls-policy.md)).
3. Transfers out of any account follow the dual-authorization rules in [FIN-001 §4.4](02-financial-controls-policy.md).
4. No director or officer may direct TENNISSA funds to an institution or product in which they or a family member have a financial interest without approval under the [Conflict of Interest Policy](07-conflict-of-interest-policy.md).

## 9. Reporting

1. The monthly financial report to the Board includes each account's balance, institution, product type and earnings, and confirms compliance with this policy.
2. The Financial Controller Director reports any breach of this policy to the Board Chair immediately.

## 10. Review

The Board reviews this policy every year and whenever federal regulations, IRS guidance, TDOE rules or Tennessee's state scholarship programs change.
